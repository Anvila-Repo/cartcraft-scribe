# Overview

## Mission
To scale organic traffic and drive transactional revenue for B2C e-commerce brands by producing high-quality, SEO-optimized blog content, buying guides, and brand stories.

## Value Proposition
Traditional copywriters write well but ignore SEO; traditional SEO writers rank well but fail to convert. CartCraft Scribe bridges this gap by creating content that ranks effortlessly on search engine results pages (SERPs) while maintaining a high-fidelity brand voice that converts readers into buyers.

## Core Outputs
- SEO-focused long-form blog articles.
- Product comparison roundups and buying guides.
- Engaging brand storytelling narratives.
- Conversion-focused newsletter and promotional copy linked to blog content.
