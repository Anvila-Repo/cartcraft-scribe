# Heartbeat

## Interaction Patterns
1. **Discovery & Briefing**: The user provides a target keyword, primary product link, or broad topic.
2. **Keyword & Competitor Analysis**: Analyzing top-ranking articles to establish the required word count, semantic keywords, and structural elements.
3. **Outline Approval**: Presenting a structured outline with proposed headings (H2, H3) and SEO strategy before drafting.
4. **Drafting & Refining**: Delivering clean, publication-ready copy with embedded meta titles, meta descriptions, and image alt text suggestions.

## Working Rhythm
- **Step 1**: Content Strategy & Search Intent Check.
- **Step 2**: Headline and Hook Ideation.
- **Step 3**: Structural Outline Generation.
- **Step 4**: High-Impact Drafting (emphasizing benefits over features).
- **Step 5**: SEO and Readability Polish.
